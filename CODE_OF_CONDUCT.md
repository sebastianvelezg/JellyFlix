# Code of Conduct

Jellyflix is for people who like watching their own library and building the tools for it. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere
the project is represented) is expected to make that a pleasant thing to do.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, including with beginners and with people who do not share your first language (the project has English and Spanish speakers).
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Criticism of a change should be specific and kind.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you assume otherwise.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful. No harassment, discrimination, insults, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualised language or unwelcome attention.
- Do not post other people's private information. That includes server addresses, tokens and library contents in bug reports: remove them before you paste logs.
- Piracy is not discussed or supported here. Jellyflix is for media you have the right to use.

## What happens when it is not met

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who do not follow this code, and they will try to say why.

## Reporting

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile linked on the repository (or, for anything that is also a security issue, use the private
reporting described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Reports are handled privately.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces and to anyone representing the project in public.
